3.2 Sign-in, Two-Factor and Token Issue 
Owning service: AuthService 
Sign-in verifies the password, records the attempt, and only then issues tokens. Two rules are 
enforced on the server rather than in the client: an account locks once consecutive failures reach the 
limit, and the platform a role may use is checked before the session is released ΓÇö a Customer is 
refused on the web and an Admin or Manager on mobile, with the tokens that were just minted 
discarded. Two-factor is TOTP-based, with an SMS one-time code and single-use backup codes as 
alternatives, and a cross-device confirmation path for approving a sign-in from an already trusted 
device.

3.3 Battery Asset and Threshold Management 
Owning service: BatteryService 
Thresholds are versioned rather than edited. Activating a new version leaves the previous one intact, 
which is what lets an alert raised months ago still be interpreted against the limits in force when it 
fired. Asset registration enforces the immutability of the serial number and the requirement that an 
asset belongs to exactly one site and one battery type.
