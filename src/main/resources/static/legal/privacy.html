SAGA Learning Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 8, 2026

Overview

SAGA Learning (“SAGA”, “we”, “us”) is an academic and project-collaboration application. This Privacy Policy describes how we process information when you use SAGA and when you connect third-party services such as Atlassian (Jira) and GitHub.

Information we may process

Depending on how you use SAGA, we may process:

  • SAGA account and profile identifiers (such as user id, email, name, and role)
  • Atlassian account identifiers associated with a connected OAuth identity
  • Jira site, project, and board metadata selected for a SAGA project
  • Jira issues/tasks and related project activity synchronized into SAGA
  • GitHub identity, repository, and commit metadata when users connect GitHub
  • Integration metadata reasonably necessary to operate connected features

How we use information

We process this information to:

  • connect external accounts that users authorize
  • synchronize Jira tasks into SAGA
  • synchronize GitHub commits into SAGA
  • attribute project activity to SAGA users
  • support team and project learning features

OAuth credentials

When you authorize an integration, OAuth access and refresh credentials may be stored securely (including encryption at rest where applicable) and used only to perform the authorized integration functionality within SAGA.

Data sharing

SAGA Learning does not sell personal data. Information may be processed by infrastructure providers that host or operate the service, and by Atlassian or GitHub when you use their APIs under your authorization. Those providers process data under their own terms and policies.

Retention

We retain information only as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain academic and project records, support security and operational requirements, and comply with applicable obligations.

Your choices

You may revoke provider authorization from Atlassian and/or GitHub account settings. You may also contact the SAGA Learning Team regarding stored integration data associated with your SAGA account.

Security

SAGA Learning uses reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information processed by the service. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Contact

For privacy questions related to SAGA Learning, contact the SAGA Learning Team through your institution’s project channel or the contact method published with your deployment.
